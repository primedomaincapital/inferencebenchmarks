AI Inference Costs & Benchmark Scores — Market Data

Inference pricing now moves faster than the benchmarks that are supposed to justify it. A flagship that led an index in September can be repriced, discounted, or undercut by October, and the cheapest model on a leaderboard is rarely the cheapest model on an invoice once output tokens are counted.

The tables below keep the two questions separate: what models score, and what they charge. The score table uses the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index as reported in public summaries; the price table uses published per-million-token list prices. Neither table, on its own, answers which model to run — that answer lives in the ratio between them.

Methodology

Included: the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index as republished in public rankings, published per-token list prices as summarized from provider pricing pages, and MLPerf Inference results as published by submitters. Excluded: negotiated enterprise discounts, which providers do not publish, and any single-vendor benchmark that is not an MLPerf or Artificial Analysis result. Per-GPU MLPerf figures are derived by submitters by dividing system throughput by accelerator count and are labelled as such in the source; system-level v6.0 figures are not directly comparable to per-GPU v5.x figures. v6.0 per-GPU figures are compared across benchmark rounds, not within one round.

Trend labels: “Baseline (October 2026) — trend tracking starts with the next refresh” means no prior-period figure from the same source was available this session, so no movement is claimed. Where a prior figure is shown, the trend compares the current figure with that prior figure from the same source.
